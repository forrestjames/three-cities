# Logo

## The mark
Three buildings, one for each city: Boston, Copenhagen and London, left to right. Drawn on a 35.4 by 24 grid, so it stays crisp from favicon to poster. One path per building.

## Files (`design-assets/logo/`)
- `skyline-ink.svg`: the mark in ink, for light grounds.
- `skyline-ground.svg`: the mark in the dark ground colour.
- `skyline-ember.svg`: the mark in the accent.
- `skyline-cities.svg`: one colour per city.
- `lockup-dark.svg`, `lockup-light.svg`, `lockup-transparent.svg`: mark plus the name, type outlined.
- `-text` versions keep the words as live text so they can be edited.

## Layout
- Centre the logo with large type below and some space. If the text is left aligned, aligning the logo left is fine.
- Give it room. Clear space of at least one building width on every side.
- Wordmark is Abel, caps, tracking 14 to 22 percent.

## Do
- Use `skyline-cities.svg` only when all three cities are in play.
- Use one flat colour everywhere else.
- Use the lockup file, not a rebuilt one.

## Don't
- Don't recolour buildings to anything outside the three city colours or ember.
- Don't stretch, skew, outline or add shadows.
- Don't put the mark on busy imagery without a calm panel behind it.
- Don't reorder the buildings. Boston, Copenhagen, London, left to right.
