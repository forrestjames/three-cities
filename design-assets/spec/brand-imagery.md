# Imagery and texture

## Feel
Artsy and tactile, not flat. Paper textures and risograph grain. Warm dark grounds with soft light.

## What is already in the app
- **Hero:** risograph grain over soft colour blobs, one blob per city.
- **Pedals:** a faint brushed stripe and a gradient from top to bottom. Hall is plum, Delay is deep green. The LED is the lit part.
- **Lamp:** warm `--color-lamp` glow for things that are on.
- **City cards:** each city in its own colour, with temperature large, then place, local time and day or night.

## Colour use
- Ground and panel for surfaces. Ink for text, ink-dim for secondary, ink-faint for hints.
- One accent at a time. Ember by default.
- City colours stay with their city. Boston terracotta, Copenhagen green, London lilac.

## Graphics (social, reels, launch)
- Centred logo, large type below, plenty of space.
- Add grain or paper texture. Avoid flat fills.
- Show real data when you can: live temperatures, real times.
- Source files and renders live in `launch-graphics/`. The library is `/graphics`.

## Photography and field recordings
Not used yet. If city field recordings or photos arrive, keep them warm, grainy and unposed, one city at a time.

## Avoid
- Generic AI dashboard looks: glassy gradients, neon on black, stock charts.
- Stock photos of skylines.
