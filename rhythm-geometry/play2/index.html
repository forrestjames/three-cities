Rhythm Geometry

Every city has a sound. This page takes drum rhythms from genres around the world, from Cuban clave to Chicago house to Lagos afrobeat, and draws each one as a ring.

Then it asks the weather. Live temperature, wind, cloud and rain from the city you pick shape the other rings, so the same place sounds a little different every hour.

Pick a city and a genre, press play, and slide the forecast forward to hear tomorrow.

Each drum has its own Delay and Reverb knobs on its strip. These two pedals shape the sound they all feed.

MIDI out

Drum notes follow the name on each fader (kick 36, snare 38, closed hat 42, open hat 46, clap 39, toms 43, 47, 50, rim 37, cymbals 49 and 51), so a TR-8S or similar plays the same drum you see in every kit. Percussion with no match goes to the nearest drum. Melodic sounds go out on the melody channel as notes in the key, with a program change that picks the closest General MIDI sound: kalimba 109, harp 47, electric piano 5, steel drums 115 (as numbered on most synths).

Every hit you hear goes out too, including stutters, chance and mutes. Knobs use CC 74 (filter), 75 (LFO level), 76 (chance). Faders use CC 7.
