About the numbers

London: 12,938 people seen sleeping rough during 2025/26 (CHAIN, Greater London Authority).

Boston: 3,674 people experiencing homelessness in a one night count, January 2026, including people in shelters (Boston Health Care for the Homeless Program).

Copenhagen: 1,572 people experiencing homelessness in a counting week, February 2026 (VIVE).

Paris: 3,857 people with no shelter solution counted in the Nuit de la Solidarité, night of 22 to 23 January 2026 (City of Paris).

New York: 4,991 people sleeping unsheltered, HOPE survey, 10 March 2026 (NYC Department of Social Services). It does not include people in shelters.

These measure different things, so please don't read them as a ranking. Dot values are rounded and shown in each ring. The dots are not real people and the houses are drawn, not photographed. The more people counted, the more hits in the ring. The sound is a soft placeholder, with a low drone under each city that gets stronger as the count rises.
