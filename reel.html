The series

  1. The hook. One post a day: "Right now it's 46°C in Kuwait City and −41°C in Yakutsk. Here's what they sound like together." The extremes change daily, so the series never repeats, which is the whole idea of the piece.
  2. The shape. 26 seconds. A title card, then each city revealed with its photos, its temperature, local time and the loop the weather made, then all three playing together as sliding strips, then the end card with the link. Photos are duotoned in each city's colour and cut on the beat, so it looks like a collage rather than an app screenshot.
  3. The ritual. Same time every day, ideally when it's morning in London and night somewhere extreme. The caption template below names the three and their numbers. Pin a post explaining the idea, and keep the format identical so it's recognisable at a glance.
  4. Variations for the week. Mondays "the three coldest". Wednesdays "where it's raining hardest". Fridays "three cities that just woke up" (use Surprise me ☀). Sundays a follower's three cities, from the comments.
  5. Invite people in. End every caption with "Comment three places you've lived and I'll play them tomorrow." That gives you Sunday's post and gets people to the site.
  6. Where it goes. Instagram Reels and TikTok as the main 9:16 video. YouTube Shorts as a mirror. A weekly carousel of stills from the reels for the grid.
