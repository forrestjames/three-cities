The series

  1. The hook. One post a day, one question a day. "Where's the best autumn break right now?" "Which three cities are at exactly 14° and living completely different days?" The answers come from the live weather, so the series never repeats.
  2. The rhythm of the week. Pick for me follows a weekly shape: Monday travel, Tuesday mood, Wednesday time of day, Thursday contrasts, Friday something playful, Saturday the extremes, Sunday a follower's cities from the comments.
  3. Fresh faces. Cities you've opened a reel with in the last two weeks sit out, so the same places don't keep coming back. Clear the list if you want them back.
  4. The shape. 26 seconds. A title card with the question, each city revealed with its photos, its number and the loop its weather made, all three playing together, then the end card with the link.
  5. Invite people in. Every caption ends by asking for three places people have lived. That's Sunday's post, and it brings people to the site.
  6. Where it goes. Instagram Reels and TikTok as the main 9:16 video. YouTube Shorts as a mirror. A weekly carousel of stills for the grid.
